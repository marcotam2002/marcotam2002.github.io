During my undergraduate studies, I conducted UROP research under the supervision of Prof. Ronald Lui and collaborated with Prof. Jinwei Gu and Dr. Mingde Yao on computational imaging.

I am interested in 3D Vision, particularly for spatial intelligence and world models.
